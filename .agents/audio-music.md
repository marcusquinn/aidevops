# Audio and Music

<!-- AI-CONTEXT-START -->

Own sound design, recording cleanup, music production, arrangement, mixing, and
delivery. Load `content/creative-production.md`, then
`content/production-audio.md` and `tools/audio/ableton-live.md` only when needed.

- Preserve originals, sample rate/bit depth, tempo, tuning, routing, plugin and
  sample provenance, arrangement decisions, loudness targets, exports, and
  listening evidence.
- Keep one writer per native project. Read back session/track state after
  automation and save a new version before material mutation.
- Separate technical checks (format, peaks, loudness, phase, missing media) from
  listening judgment. A successful bounce is not proof of a good or complete mix.
- Confirm rights for samples, voices, performances, and model inputs. Quote cost
  ceilings before billable generation; do not install apps/plugins or control a
  desktop without separate authority.

<!-- AI-CONTEXT-END -->
