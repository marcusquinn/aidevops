# Creative Intelligence

Use `creative-intelligence-helper.py analyze --input SNAPSHOT --decisions LABELS --dry-run` for offline, evidence-linked recommendations. It labels hooks, format, offer, awareness and audience fit; deduplicates concepts by substantive labels rather than superficial variants; and groups owned outcomes by audience, placement and window.

- Competitor Ad Library longevity is an observation, never evidence of ROAS or profit.
- Fatigue produces leave, refresh or review hypotheses only after the snapshot's stated window, sample and attribution lag; it retains confounders and never pauses ads.
- Policy and UGC checks retain unknown visual-product, disclosure and rights evidence. Transcript-only evidence cannot satisfy those requirements, and precheck never certifies provider approval.
- The output is recommendation-only and emits a typed landing-matcher handoff until the shared matcher is available.

Use the established creative brief, testing, UGC and production QA documents for human review of the resulting evidence.
