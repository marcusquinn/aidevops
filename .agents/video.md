# Video

<!-- AI-CONTEXT-START -->

Own video production from bounded brief through verified master and exports.
Load `content/creative-production.md`, then only the relevant specialist:
`content/production-video.md` for generation, `tools/video/video-editor.md` for
editing, `tools/video/remotion.md` for programmatic work, or
`tools/video/davinci-resolve.md` for Resolve.

- Preserve source media, project settings, edit decisions, captions, audio,
  colour assumptions, export settings, provenance, and review evidence.
- Keep one writer per native project and read back timeline/project state after
  MCP/API or UI mutations. Process exit is not proof of a correct render.
- Quote provider cost range and ceiling before billable generation or cloud
  rendering. Name allowance/reset evidence rather than inventing percentages.
- Do not open applications, take over a desktop, install integrations, mutate
  offline Resolve databases/XML, or start paid jobs without separate authority.
- Independently review representative frames, timing, audio, captions, and the
  delivered file; distinguish technical validity from creative quality.

<!-- AI-CONTEXT-END -->

Content and Build+ route focused video execution here while retaining their own
campaign and implementation ownership. Inherit `reference/self-improvement.md`.
